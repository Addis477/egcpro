Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous experience?
No prior experience is required. Our courses are designed for beginners and professionals alike. We start with fundamentals and progress to advanced techniques.
Is the certification internationally recognized?
Yes! Our certification is recognized by leading gemological institutions worldwide and valued by international buyers and employers.
What equipment will I learn on?
You'll train on professional-grade equipment including refractometers, spectroscopes, microscopes, faceting machines, and polishing equipment from Germany and Italy.
Can I start my own business after completion?
Absolutely! Many of our graduates have started successful gemstone export businesses. Our business module covers market access, pricing, and international trade.
Are there payment plans available?
Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. Contact our admissions team to discuss options that work for your budget.
What makes EGC Global different?
We have foreign geologists on faculty, state-of-the-art equipment, direct industry connections, and a focus on Ethiopian gemstones specifically.

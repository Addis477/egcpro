Ethiopia's
Gemstone Legacy

Ethiopia is home to some of the world's most spectacular gemstones – from mesmerizing Welo opals to rare emeralds, sapphires, and garnets. Our country sits on a treasure trove of mineral wealth waiting to be discovered, refined, and shared with the world.

The EGC Global Training Centre, in partnership with international geologists and gemologists, brings world-class education to Ethiopian entrepreneurs, miners, and enthusiasts.

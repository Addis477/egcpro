Ethiopia's Gemstone Legacy

Ethiopia is home to some of the world's most spectacular gemstones – from mesmerizing opals to rare emeralds, sapphires, and garnets. The EGC Global Training Centre, in partnership with international geologists and gemologists, brings world-class education to Ethiopian entrepreneurs and miners.

Our mission is to empower local talent with the skills to identify, cut, and export Ethiopian gemstones to global markets. With state-of-the-art facilities and expert foreign instructors, we are creating a new generation of certified gemologists.
